# Case Study: UPI / Online Payment Fraud

## Type of Cybercrime
**UPI/Online Payment Fraud** — also known as Digital Payment Fraud or UPI Scam

---

## What is UPI Fraud?

UPI (Unified Payments Interface) fraud refers to any deceptive activity in which a
criminal exploits India's UPI-based digital payment system to illegally transfer money
from a victim's bank account. As UPI adoption has grown dramatically in India — with
over 13 billion transactions per month in 2024 — it has also become a major target for
cybercriminals.

---

## A Realistic Scenario: The "Request Money" Scam

**Background:**
Arjun, a 19-year-old first-year engineering student in Bhopal, listed his old bicycle for
sale on an online marketplace. He received a call from "Ravi," who claimed to be
interested in buying it.

**Step-by-Step: How the Fraud Happened**

1. Ravi told Arjun he would send payment via Google Pay and asked for his UPI ID.
2. Arjun shared his UPI ID without suspicion.
3. Ravi sent a **UPI Collect Request (Request Money)** for Rs. 5,000 instead of
   sending payment. The notification said: *"Ravi wants to pay you Rs. 5,000 – Approve?"*
4. Arjun, confused by the wording, thought approving the request would receive the
   money. He entered his UPI PIN and approved it.
5. Rs. 5,000 was instantly debited from Arjun's account and transferred to Ravi.
6. Ravi disconnected the call and became unreachable.

**Key Deception:**
The fraudster exploited a common misunderstanding: on UPI apps, a **Collect Request
requires the victim to enter their PIN — which authorises an outgoing payment, not an
incoming one.** Legitimate payments never require the recipient to enter their PIN.

---

## Who is Usually Targeted?

- Students and young adults using UPI for the first time
- People selling items on second-hand marketplaces (OLX, Facebook Marketplace)
- Small business owners and daily wage workers
- Elderly users unfamiliar with digital payment systems

---

## Consequences

- Immediate financial loss (often Rs. 5,000 – Rs. 50,000 or more)
- Psychological distress and loss of trust in digital payments
- Difficulty recovering funds (UPI transactions are often irreversible)
- In severe cases, victims may be manipulated into multiple transactions
