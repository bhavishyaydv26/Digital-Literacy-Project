# Stay Safe Online – Prevention Checklist
## For College Students in India

---

### 🔐 General Digital Safety

1. **Use strong, unique passwords** – Create a password of at least 12 characters with
   a mix of uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and symbols. Never use your name, date of
   birth, or "password123".

2. **Enable Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)** – Turn on 2FA on all important accounts
   (email, social media, banking apps). Even if someone steals your password, they
   cannot access your account without the second factor.

3. **Keep your apps and phone software updated** – Security updates patch known
   vulnerabilities. Set your phone to auto-update apps and the operating system.

4. **Do not click on suspicious links** – Whether in SMS, WhatsApp, or email — if you
   didn't ask for it and it looks urgent, verify before clicking. Phishing links often
   mimic trusted brands (banks, NPCI, Amazon, etc.).

5. **Lock your phone with a strong PIN or biometric** – Avoid using simple patterns or
   4-digit PINs. Use a 6-digit PIN or fingerprint/face unlock.

6. **Do not share personal information on unknown websites** – Your Aadhaar number,
   PAN number, and date of birth are sensitive. Only enter them on official government
   or verified websites.

---

### 💳 UPI & Financial Safety (Mandatory for Indian Students)

7. **ENTERING your PIN means SENDING money – never the opposite** – If someone
   asks you to enter your UPI PIN to "receive" money, it is a scam. You never need to
   enter your PIN to receive a payment. Your PIN is only for authorising payments YOU
   make.

8. **Never share your UPI PIN, OTP, or CVV with anyone** – No bank, UPI app, or
   payment company will ever call you asking for this information. Hang up immediately
   if someone asks.

---

### 🧠 Awareness & Behaviour

9. **Verify seller/buyer identity before UPI transactions** – For marketplace transactions,
   speak with the person on video call if possible before transferring any money.

10. **Do not install unknown apps from links shared via SMS or WhatsApp** – Only
    install apps from the official Google Play Store or Apple App Store.

11. **Be careful on public Wi-Fi** – Avoid doing banking, shopping, or logging into
    important accounts when using public Wi-Fi at cafes, malls, or college common areas.
    Use a VPN if necessary.

---

### 🚨 Reporting: What to Do If You Are a Victim

12. **Report immediately on the National Cyber Crime Portal:**
    🌐 **cybercrime.gov.in**

13. **Call the National Cyber Crime Helpline:**
    📞 **1930** (Available 24×7, toll-free)

14. **File a complaint with your nearest police station** and keep a copy of the FIR.

15. **Contact your bank immediately** to block your account or UPI if you suspect
    unauthorised transactions. Time is critical — act within the first few hours.
